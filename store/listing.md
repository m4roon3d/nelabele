## Full description (max 4000)

English:

NelaBele (ನೆಲ ಬೆಲೆ, "land price") shows what land and homes cost across Bengaluru Urban, Bengaluru Rural,
Ramanagara, Tumakuru, Kolar and Chikkaballapura.

For every village, layout and locality you get:
• The government guidance value, the minimum used for registration, for plots, houses, flats and farmland
• A market range from published reports, auction reserves and listing averages, with a confidence level and price history
• Typical rents and rental yield, where reliable data exists
• The nearest metro station and ring road

Tools for buyers:
• Is this price fair? Put an asking price against the guidance value and market range
• Total cost: stamp duty, cess, surcharge and registration on any price
• Home loan and EMI: monthly instalment from a price, or the price your budget can buy
• House value: land plus what the building would cost today, less age
• Buyer's checklist: khata, e-Swathu, conversion, RERA and the papers to ask for

Keep track:
• Save places and compare two or three side by side
• See what has changed for your saved places since you last looked
• Add your own notes, contacts and photos of documents to a place. They stay on your phone.

My property:
• Keep your property ID, e-khata and ward handy, and open the official GBA tax site to pay
• Record each year's tax with a photo of the receipt
• A yearly reminder for the 5% early-payment rebate, and a rough tax estimate
• Village properties: where to pay the gram panchayat

Also: a map coloured by price, search in English or Kannada, places near you, sharing, light and dark themes,
and the whole app in Kannada.

Rates are indicative, not valuations or advice. Always check documents with the sub-registrar and a lawyer before you pay.
