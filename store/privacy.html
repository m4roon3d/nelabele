NelaBele privacy policy

Last updated 19 November 2026

NelaBele (ನೆಲ ಬೆಲೆ) shows indicative property rates and government guidance values for Bengaluru and the surrounding districts. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics or tracking.

What leaves your phone

  • Searches and the places you open. The app asks our database (hosted by Neon) for rates. These requests carry what you searched for or the place you opened, and nothing that identifies you.
  • Your location, only when you tap "Places near me". The app sends your coordinates once to find nearby places. They are used for that request only and are not stored. "Show where I am" on the map uses your location on the phone only.
  • Map tiles come from OpenFreeMap, which sees the map area you are viewing, as any map does.
  • Market rates you log with an editor key (owner only) are saved to the database with the place, rate, date and source you enter.

What stays on your phone

  • Saved places, your language and theme choice, and the last state of your saved places (for "what's changed").
  • Notes, contacts and document photos you add to a place. These are never uploaded. They are removed if you uninstall the app (or clear the browser's data, on the web version).
  • The editor key, if you add one.

Permissions

  • Location: only to find places near you, when you ask.
  • Camera and photos: only when you add a document photo to a place; the photo stays on the phone.
  • Internet: to fetch rates and maps.

Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data. When you use "Share this place", you choose the app and the people it goes to.

Children

NelaBele is meant for adults looking at property and is not directed at children.

About the rates

Rates are indicative, from government guidance values and published reports. They are not valuations or advice.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue at github.com/m4roon3d/nelabele/issues.
